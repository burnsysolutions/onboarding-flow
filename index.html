How it fits Adrageous

The face-to-face stays. The chasing goes.

Meeting clients on site and Benito's sign-off stay exactly as they are. The automation takes the set-up, chasing and admin in between, inside the tools the team already uses, so every client gets the same complete start.

What it takes off Benito

Chasing

Logins, logos, photos and answers, chased by text until they are in.

Setting up

ClickUp projects, folders and client records, built the moment a client signs.

Briefing

Each specialist gets their tasks and the client's answers without a handover call.

Remembering

A live checklist per client shows what is done and what is still missing.

Who does what

People do the parts clients value

The automation

Welcomes, collects, chases, builds the workspace, connects the forms and reports.

The client

Fills in one form, grants access from one link and books the site visit.

The team

Visits the site, films the work, builds the campaigns and sets up tracking.

Benito

Signs the client, approves the launch and spends the time saved on selling.

What stays the same

  • The site visit and meeting the people doing the work.
  • No set packages: each client still gets what is worth doing.
  • ClickUp and Zapier, the tools already in use.
  • Nothing goes live without Benito's yes.

What changes

  • Onboarding starts the minute a client signs.
  • Nobody chases by hand.
  • Every client has starting numbers to beat.
  • Benito sees one message per client, not twenty.

What it adds

Better proof for every case study

Because the starting enquiry numbers are recorded for every client, each monthly report shows a real before and after: enquiries, cost per enquiry and, where the client's records allow, jobs won. That turns every new client into a case study with timeframes and figures.

Tools and set-up

What it runs on, and how it is switched on

Built on ClickUp and Zapier, plus a few small tools only where they add something. Everything runs in Adrageous's own accounts, and logins are never sent by text or email.

The way

Switching it on, in order

  1. Walk through today's onboarding with Benito, step by step, and time it.
  2. Build the intake form, welcome messages and ClickUp templates.
  3. Add the access link and the text reminders.
  4. Add tracking, lead alerts and the ready check.
  5. Run the next new client through it, then the next three, and compare with today.

Six things to settle

1 · The CRMWhich one is used today, and whether it takes new records automatically.
2 · The triggerWhich tools handle proposals and payments.
3 · TemplatesOne ClickUp template per service, with the right owner on each task.
4 · Texts or WhatsAppWhich one clients answer fastest.
5 · Call trackingThe provider, and whether it is already in use.
6 · SecurityAccess granted through the platforms themselves, never passwords by message.
